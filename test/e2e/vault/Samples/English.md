# At the river

The morning ferry leaves the small pier at eight. Mira carries a notebook and a blue umbrella. Across the river, the market is already open.

She writes down the names of three birds.
The first waits on the rail.
The second circles above the boat.
The third disappears into the reeds.
She draws the shape of its wings.
She studies the water.
A ripple moves past the boat.
The wind turns a page.
She holds the paper flat.
Then she closes the notebook.

At the market, a baker gives her a warm roll. She sits beside the fountain and reads the letter from her sister.

The last boat leaves before sunset. Mira puts the letter in her coat and walks back to the pier.
